# Model 2 — Unsupervised Learning: Reading Material

Covers: K-Means clustering, PCA, dimensionality reduction, clustering evaluation.

## Core concepts
- **StatQuest — K-means clustering** and **StatQuest — PCA main ideas**
  (YouTube): the clearest short explanations of both topics available for free.
  https://www.youtube.com/@statquest
- **An Introduction to Statistical Learning**, Ch. 12 (unsupervised learning —
  covers PCA and clustering directly): https://www.statlearning.com/
- **scikit-learn User Guide — Clustering** (official docs, includes a visual
  comparison of clustering algorithms): https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/clustering.html
- **scikit-learn User Guide — Decomposing signals (PCA)**:
  https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/decomposition.html#pca

## Why PCA works (the linear algebra intuition)
- **3Blue1Brown — Essence of Linear Algebra**, specifically the videos on
  eigenvectors/eigenvalues and change of basis — PCA is a direct application
  of both. https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZHQObOWTQDPD3MizzM2xVFitgF8hE_ab

## Evaluating clusters (harder than it sounds — there's no ground truth)
- **scikit-learn — Clustering performance evaluation** (silhouette score,
  and why you often can't just "check accuracy"):
  https://scikit-learn.org/stable/modules/clustering.html#clustering-performance-evaluation
